
# 🍔 Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Swiggy sales data using **SQL, Excel/CSV, and Power BI** to identify business insights related to sales performance, restaurants, customers, delivery operations, and profitability.

The project contains **5 interactive Power BI dashboards** designed to help understand business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🌐 Live Project

👉 [View Swiggy Sales Analysis Project](https://puneeths602-cpu.github.io/Swiggy-Sales-Analysis/)

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total sales and revenue performance
- Identify top-performing restaurants
- Analyze customer ordering behavior
- Evaluate delivery performance
- Compare city-wise sales performance
- Analyze food category and payment trends
- Track monthly orders, revenue, profit, and discounts
- Generate actionable business insights

## 📈 Dashboards

### 1️⃣ Executive Summary

Provides an overall view of business performance.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Rating
- Total Profit
- Top 10 Restaurants by Revenue
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by City
- Revenue by Food Category
- Payment Method Distribution
- Order Status Distribution

### 2️⃣ Restaurant Analysis

Analyzes restaurant-level performance including:

- Restaurant Revenue
- Restaurant Profit
- Restaurant Ratings
- Restaurant Orders
- Top Performing Restaurants
- Restaurant-wise comparisons

### 3️⃣ Delivery Analysis

Focuses on delivery operations:

- Delivery Partner Performance
- Delivery Time
- Delivery Distance
- Delivery Fee
- Delivery Success Rate
- Delivery Performance Analysis

### 4️⃣ Customer Analysis

Analyzes customer behavior:

- Customer Ratings
- Payment Methods
- City-wise Customer Analysis
- Area-wise Analysis
- Customer Orders
- Customer Behavior Trends

### 5️⃣ Business Insights

Provides high-level business insights:

- Monthly Profit Trend
- Monthly Orders Trend
- Discount Analysis
- Profit Percentage
- Revenue Performance
- Business KPI Summary
